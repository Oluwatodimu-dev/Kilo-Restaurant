What we collect

When you book a table, place an order, contact us or subscribe, we collect the details you enter (name, phone, email, notes) only to fulfil your request and communicate with you.

How we use it

We use your information to confirm bookings and orders, answer questions and, if you subscribe, send occasional offers. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email. We do not sell your data.

Storage in your browser

Your current order, saved dishes and theme preference are kept in your own browser so they are not lost when you refresh. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

Orders & allergies

Please tell us about any allergies when ordering. Our kitchen handles nuts, sesame, gluten, dairy and eggs and cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment.

Your rights

Contact us using the details on our website to access, correct or delete your information.
